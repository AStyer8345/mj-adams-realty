## Tone translation: Instagram → Website

Her Instagram is emoji-heavy, hashtag-loaded, scripture-forward, bold-unicode fonts. **The website is the polished channel** — keep the heart, strip the noise.

| Keep | Drop |
|------|------|
| Warmth, "matching hearts with homes," HomeMatchMaker | Emojis (🤍🙏🏡) |
| Relationship-over-transaction framing | Hashtags |
| Her leap-of-faith / transition story | Bold-unicode lettering |
| Relocation empathy ("I made the move too") | Full scripture blocks |
| A tasteful, genuine faith nod | Sermon-length devotionals |

**Open judgment call: how heavy to go on faith.** Her brand is genuinely faith-centered — erasing it would be inauthentic. But a homepage of scripture narrows her market. Recommended calibration: **one sincere line** (e.g., "Faith-driven, people-first") rather than scripture quotes. Confirm with Adam before finalizing.
