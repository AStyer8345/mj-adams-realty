About MJ

Meet
MJ Adams

Marjory — MJ to everyone — was born and raised in Houston. In 2016 she and her family took a leap of faith and moved to Austin, fell for it instantly, and never looked back.

Before real estate, she spent nearly a decade as a stay-at-home mom, building a health & nutrition coaching business from her kitchen table. The work ethic — and the habit of putting people first — came with her into real estate in 2022.

Since then she's earned a place in the top 1.5% of agents nationally, been featured in Austin Real Producers Magazine, and built a reputation her clients all describe the same way: constant communication, real care, and someone who negotiates hard for your outcome.

She calls it matching hearts with homes — faith first, people always. And because she made the move herself, she doesn't just find you a house. She shows you why you'll stay.

Why
Austin?

MJ documents her life here on social media for one reason: to show people why Austin is an excellent place to live. The short version:

The Outdoors

Weekends outside are the whole point. She'll tell you which neighborhoods put the trails, the water, and the green space closest to your door.

The Food & Nightlife

She keeps a running list of restaurants and night-out spots — and she shares it freely with every client who lands here.

The Fitness Culture

A former health & nutrition coach, she found her people in this city's gyms, studios, and trail groups. You will too.

Friday Night Lights

Fall means Lake Travis football. She's a top sponsor at the high school, and her kids play under those lights.
